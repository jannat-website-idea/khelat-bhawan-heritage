4. Design Principles & Layout Architecture

Editorial Split Grid 12-column responsive layout (5 cols content + 7 cols visual) with alternating Left/Right rhythm to eliminate blank side space.
Glassmorphism Accents Frosted ivory glass surfaces (backdrop-filter: blur(10px)) paired with hairline gold borders and 4-corner ornamental brackets.
FastStart Video Hero Cinematic streams opening directly with the bride ascending the grand staircase (0:00) with instant mobile autoplay.
Bengali Filigree Motifs Traditional Alpana artwork integrated into section dividers and subtle archival background watermarks.
